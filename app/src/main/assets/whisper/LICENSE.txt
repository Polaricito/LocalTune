whisper.cpp
===========

This app bundles a build of whisper.cpp (https://github.com/ggerganov/whisper.cpp)
version 1.9.5, including its ggml CPU backend.

Source: app/src/main/cpp/whisper.cpp
License: MIT (see below)

The bundled speech model `ggml-tiny-q5_1.bin` is a quantized conversion of
OpenAI's Whisper "tiny" multilingual model, distributed under the MIT license.
