A modern, fully offline music player for Android.

ArchiveTune plays the music already stored on your device. It has no internet permission, no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your library, playlists, listening history and lyrics stay on your phone.

Features:
<ul>
<li>Browse songs, albums and artists from your local library</li>
<li>Create and manage local playlists with custom covers</li>
<li>Background playback with a media session and queue controls</li>
<li>Shuffle, repeat, crossfade and sleep timer</li>
<li>EBU R128 loudness normalization</li>
<li>Tempo, pitch and playback speed controls</li>
<li>System equalizer and spatial audio integration</li>
<li>Synced lyrics from audio tags or .lrc files next to your songs</li>
<li>On-device lyrics transcription with whisper.cpp</li>
<li>Batch lyrics generation for the whole library or a single playlist</li>
<li>Home screen widgets and an always-on-display player</li>
<li>Material 3 interface with dynamic album-art colors</li>
<li>Multiple player styles, backgrounds, gestures and animations</li>
<li>Local backup and restore of app data</li>
<li>No network access, no analytics and no advertising</li>
</ul>
